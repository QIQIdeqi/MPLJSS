# Blast Flask impact

`grenade_blast_24f.png` uses [PixelFX Grenade](https://github.com/winkidney/pixelfx-godot/blob/620f7556f13be39724998ef5cb2157074d5940c1/godot/addons/pixelfx/fx/explosions/grenade.gd), commit `620f7556f13be39724998ef5cb2157074d5940c1`. It preserves the preset's blast, fire, sparks and smoke. The palette is warmer and casing fragments are tinted like stained glass. The atlas starts with the blast because the game supplies the bottle flight and its landing.

The transparent 6 × 4 atlas contains 300 × 200 pixel frames. The ground anchor is (150, 170).
